Key Features
How the Portal Works
Three-tier system ensuring every complaint is filed, tracked, resolved and verified with full accountability.
📝

Easy Complaint Filing

Students register, login and file complaints with photo evidence in minutes. Auto-generated date and branch-wise categorisation.

🔔

Real-Time Tracking

Track complaint status live — Pending, In Progress or Resolved — with teacher remarks and updated post-fix photos.

🏫

Infrastructure Inventory

Complete branch-wise room records: benches, computers, fans, projectors, electrical and window conditions.

⚡

6-Hour SLA Enforcement

Principal gets automatic alerts for complaints pending over 6 hours. Students also see their overdue complaints highlighted.

🔒

Secure & Verified

SHA-256 hashed passwords. Unique IDs enforced. Principal approval required before any student or teacher can login.

📊

Role Dashboards

Dedicated dashboards with stats, tables and tabs for Students, Teachers and Principal — each seeing only what's relevant.
